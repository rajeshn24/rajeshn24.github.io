Mastery, Agility & Design Leadership

STUNFOX INTERNATIONAL serves as the central governing umbrella for six specialized apparel and fashion brands. Embodying the speed, precision, and sharp presence depicted in our signature running fox logo, we engineer brand equity across every vertical of modern style.

Headquartered in Chickballapur, Karnataka, STUNFOX oversees strategic brand incubation, textile research, supply chain optimization, and global retail expansion for all subsidiary entities.
